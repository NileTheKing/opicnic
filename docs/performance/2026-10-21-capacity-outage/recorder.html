Tell me about a park you often go to. What does it look like, and what do you usually do there?

I start with a short run along the river, maybe thirty minutes or so. After that, I grab a coffee from one of the food trucks and just sit on a bench for a while. Sometimes I read a book, and sometimes I just watch people walking their dogs. The reason I love this park is that it helps me relax.
